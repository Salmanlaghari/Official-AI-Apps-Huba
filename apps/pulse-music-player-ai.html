⚡ Latest Release Notes (v1.16.4)

Released on: August 24, 2026

## Root cause found via field evidence

A device running the PREVIOUS build synced 577 Desi Hits songs and played them smoothly (screenshot proof). The v1.16.2/v1.16.3 parallel endpoint race was the regression: firing 4 hosts at once for every one of ~25 catalog queries (~100 rapid requests) tripped Cloudflare rate-limiting on real devices and killed the entire sync.

## What changed (PR #82)

1. **Reverted to the proven gentle sequential flow**: primary → official → mirror, 2 attempts each, 300ms inter-attempt delays, circuit breaker retained — exactly the behaviour that synced 577 songs on the working device

2. **Duplicate songs removed**: second-level catalog dedup by normalised title+artist — re-releases / 'From ' variants no longer appear 2–3× in the list (screenshot showed 'Gehra Hua' ×2, 'Tum Hi Ho' ×2)

3. Playback stream live-validation retained (dead 404 URLs still can't reach the player)

## Also included from earlier builds

  • •PagalWorld 500+ catalog (lazy streams, plays as PagalWorld), multi-pass search, tab order
  • •Single-tap Grant Access, media notification permission
  • •Remote endpoint config (hot-swap without app update)
